# Ad-Hoc-MySQL-Project-AtliQ-Hardware
MySQL-based ad-hoc sales analytics project for AtliQ Hardware. Developed SQL queries and stored procedures to analyze product, customer, market, regional, and forecast performance. Generated actionable insights through monthly sales reports, top-performer analysis, market classification, and forecast accuracy reporting.


# SQL Ad-Hoc Project

## Introduction

**AtliQ Hardware** is a fictional company that manufactures and distributes electronic hardware products across India. This project focuses on using **MySQL** to analyze the company’s sales, customer, product, market, and forecasting data.

## Problem Statement

The project involves a dataset containing multiple tables representing different aspects of AtliQ Hardware’s business operations. The objective is to develop SQL queries and stored procedures that answer key business questions and generate meaningful insights.

The analysis is designed to provide **quick, accurate, and actionable information** to business stakeholders, enabling them to monitor sales performance, evaluate customers and products, assess market performance, and make data-driven decisions.

## Business Requirements

* Generate a **monthly product-level sales report** for Amazon India customers for FY 2021 to track individual product sales performance.

* Generate an **aggregate monthly gross sales report** for Croma India to evaluate the customer’s contribution to AtliQ Hardware’s overall sales and support effective customer relationship management.

* Develop a **stored procedure for monthly gross sales reports**, allowing users to generate reports dynamically without manually modifying SQL queries or depending on the analytics team.

* Create a **stored procedure to determine the market badge** based on total sold quantity:

  * **Gold:** Total sold quantity greater than 5 million
  * **Silver:** Total sold quantity of 5 million or less

* Develop a **stored procedure to identify top customers** based on sales performance.

* Develop a **stored procedure to identify top-performing products** based on relevant sales metrics.

* Develop a **stored procedure to identify top-performing markets** based on sales performance.

* Generate an **aggregate forecast accuracy report** for all customers for a selected fiscal year to evaluate the accuracy of sales forecasts.

* Develop a **stored procedure to identify the top-performing markets within each region**.

* Develop a **stored procedure to identify the top products in each division based on sold quantity**.
